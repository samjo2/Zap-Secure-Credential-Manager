Zap ⚡

A credential manager that cannot read your secrets.

Create a vault Unlock your vault

Per-user encryption

Every credential is sealed with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from your master password. Nothing stored on disk can decrypt your vault without it.

Auto-locking sessions

Your vault key lives only in memory and is discarded after inactivity or restart. Walk away and Zap locks itself.

Tamper-evident history

Every account event is chained into a keyed hash log. Any edit or deletion of history breaks the chain and shows up on your audit page.

Strong by default

Built-in password generator, strength feedback, login throttling, and CSRF protection on every action.
